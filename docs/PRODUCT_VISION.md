# Product Vision

## One-line

CommunityOS is a consent-driven Society Intelligence platform that turns a residential community into a queryable, privacy-aware **Community Graph**—connecting residents, households, geography, professions, businesses, skills, and opportunities so the society can grow more connected and prosperous.

## What this is

A structured digital representation of an entire society and its human, professional, business, and geographic resources:

- Society management + household database  
- Professional & talent networking  
- Local business discovery  
- Community social network (feed, groups, events)  
- GIS / Society Intelligence Map  
- Community analytics & prosperity decision support  

## What this is not

- Not merely an HOA/property-management dashboard  
- Not a generic CRM  
- Not a public white-pages directory  
- Not a WhatsApp replacement  
- Not a social feed with bolted-on admin tables  

## North-star outcomes

With properly collected and permissioned data, authorized users should eventually answer:

- Who lives here, in which household/property?  
- What professions, skills, and industries exist—and where?  
- Which businesses are resident-owned; who is hiring or open to work?  
- Who can mentor, volunteer, or provide local services?  
- What gaps exist (searched skills with no supply)?  
- How is engagement, connectivity, and opportunity changing over time?  

## Product principle

The database is not for record-keeping alone. It exists so the society can understand **who, where, what, what skills/businesses/resources, what needs, who can help whom**—and act on aggregated, permissioned insight without exposing sensitive household or minor data.

## Privacy stance

Sensitive household information must never become an unrestricted public directory. Visibility is field-level, consent-driven, and server-enforced. Minors/dependents are private by default and excluded from discovery surfaces. Geographic precision degrades by permission; heatmaps aggregate with minimum thresholds.

## Long-term modules (conceptual)

| Pillar | Intent |
|--------|--------|
| Society Ops | Geography, properties, households, admin |
| Resident Graph | Profiles, employment, education, skills, intents |
| Talent Directory | Profession/skill search with privacy filters |
| Business Network | Resident businesses, directory, map, hiring |
| Prosperity Hub | Jobs, internships, mentoring, volunteering |
| Community Social | Feed, groups, events, announcements, polls |
| Messaging | DM + group chat with realtime UX |
| Intelligence | Dashboards, maps, gaps, prosperity indicators |
| GIS Map | Signature interactive geographic experience |

## Success metrics (directional)

- Profile completeness & verification freshness  
- Permissioned professional/business discovery usage  
- Connection / mentorship / opportunity match rates  
- Event and group engagement  
- Resident-owned business discovery  
- Zero tolerance incidents for address/minor leakage  

Treat “prosperity index” style metrics as **decision-support indicators**, not absolute proof of prosperity.
